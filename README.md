## 🌸 About the Series

**Chiikawa** is a Japanese manga and anime created by the illustrator ***Nagano***.
It started as short comic strips posted on X (formerly Twitter) in **2020**, and was
adapted into an anime by studio *Doga Kobo* in **2022**.

The story follows tiny, round creatures who live simple lives: they work odd jobs,
eat delicious food, and study for the *weed-pulling certification exam*. But don't be
fooled by the cute art — their world also has scary monsters, strange curses, genocide, torture, kidnapping and
moments that are surprisingly ~~light-hearted~~ **emotional**.

## 🐾 Main Characters

| Character | Appearance | Personality |
|-----------|------------|-------------|
| **Chiikawa** | Small white bear-like creature | Shy, gentle, cries easily, but brave when it matters |
| **Hachiware** | White-and-blue cat with a split pattern | Cheerful, optimistic, loves playing guitar |
| **Usagi** | Yellow rabbit | Chaotic, energetic, famous for its loud *"Una!"* and *"Yaha!"* |
| **Momonga** | Flying squirrel | Wants attention and to be called cute, often bratty |
| **Kurimanju** | Chestnut-bun-like creature | Calm, laid-back, enjoys a good drink and snack |
| **Rakko** | Sea otter | A strong, respected monster-hunter that everyone admires |
